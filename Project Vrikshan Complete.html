Project Vrikshan

Building the world's largest platform for green education, sustainable farming, and carbon credit generation. This project aims to establish the world's largest integrated platform for green education, sustainable farming, afforestation, and carbon credit generation—empowering students, farmers, and communities through innovation, environmental leadership, and climate-conscious development.

🌿 What are Carbon Credits ?

Carbon credits are verified units that represent the reduction of one metric ton of CO₂ emissions through sustainable practices like afforestation. Carbon Credits are used for stabilising the harmful effect of Carbon Footprint
Project Vrikshan leverages this model by planting and tracking trees that actively absorb carbon, converting ecological restoration into verified carbon assets with recurring financial value.
These credits can be traded or sold to organizations seeking to offset their emissions, aligning with global ESG goals

Vrikshan empowers schools, communities, and sponsors to participate in a scalable green economy, where every tree planted contributes to both ecological restoration and financial value.

🏭 Business Model – Step-by-Step Execution 🏭

Step 1: Develop high-quality trees through in-house nurseries or expert collaborations.
Step 2: Distribute free plants to farmers, sign O2 rights MoUs, collect GPS/tree data, and tag trees with O2 Cards.
Step 3: Offer tiered support plans: Normal (free plant), Premium (safety cage), Diamond (fencing + irrigation).
Step 4: Monetize via O2 Cards—sell carbon credits B2C and B2B with a noble environmental brand.
Step 5: Launch a tech-enabled carbon marketplace with real-time tracking, verified credits, and community impact tokens.

Key Features

Digital Platform / Marketplace

A scalable marketplace connecting buyers and sellers of carbon credits. Enables real-time pricing, transparent transactions, and enterprise-grade integration—built for growth and global reach.

Individual (B2C) and Verified (B2B) Carbon Credits

Personal carbon offset wallets, linked to Carbon Footprint (Individual). Certified credits via global or national carbon standards (Verra, Gold Standard, India CCTS).

🛰️ AI & Satellite Verification (Future Scope).

Advanced validation layer using satellite imagery and AI models to verify carbon offset activities on the ground. Enables automated monitoring, fraud detection, and real-time impact assessment, reducing reliance on manual audits and boosting trust among stakeholders.

About Project Vrikshan

In the Vrikshan Green Club Project, we don’t just plant trees—we plant transformation. Our mission is rooted in impact: empowering communities, restoring ecosystems, and redefining how carbon credits are created and valued. We combine grassroots action with cutting-edge technology to build India’s first direct-to-consumer carbon credit platform, backed by real trees, real data, and real people. We believe the future of sustainability lies in education, transparency, and scale. That’s why we engage students through Green Clubs, train rural women and youth as field agents, and build digital tools that track every tree from soil to satellite. Our O2 Cards aren’t just tokens—they’re proof of purpose.
